Belknap County, NH Real Estate Market Report

February brought steady winter activity to Belknap County. The market posted 20 closed sales, a $504,950 median sold price, and a 97.88% sold-price-to-list-price ratio. Three-bedroom homes represented the largest share of closed sales, while two- and three-bedroom homes sold above listed price on average.

Market Takeaway

A quick read before the numbers.

Belknap County’s February market suggests that buyer demand remained selective but resilient. Homes sold at 97.88% of listed price overall, with 2-bedroom and 3-bedroom homes performing especially well against their listed prices. Larger homes still achieved meaningful activity, but the longer market time in the 4-bedroom and 5+ bedroom categories points to a more patient, price-sensitive segment.

What This Means for Sellers

  • Pricing discipline matters: homes sold at 97.88% of listed price countywide.
  • Three-bedroom homes remained the main demand driver, with 11 closed sales.
  • Preparation and positioning are especially important for larger homes, where days on market can stretch.

What This Means for Buyers

  • Move decisively on well-priced homes: 2-bedroom and 3-bedroom homes sold above listed price on average.
  • Median DOM of 34 days suggests that buyers had some time, but strong homes still required focus.
  • Higher-bedroom segments may offer more room for strategy, particularly when homes have been on the market longer.

Key Market Highlights

  • 20 closed sales out of 63 total listings.
  • $504,950 median sold price countywide.
  • 3-bedroom homes led the month with 11 sales and a median sold price of $434,900.
  • 2-bedroom homes achieved the strongest sold-price-to-list-price ratio at 102.11%.
  • 18 expired listings indicate that pricing strategy still mattered in the February market.
