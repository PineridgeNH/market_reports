Market Takeaway

A quick read before the numbers.

Belknap County saw a balanced spring-market mix in March: inventory expanded, but well-positioned homes still sold close to asking. The standout category was 3-bedroom homes, with 34 closed sales, while 2-bedroom homes sold slightly above list price on average. Larger homes continued to require more patience, especially in the 4+ bedroom segments.

What This Means for Sellers

  • Strong pricing still works: homes sold at 98.12% of list price countywide.
  • 3-bedroom homes remain the primary demand driver in Belknap County.
  • Presentation and pricing are especially important in larger-home categories where market time can stretch.

What This Means for Buyers

  • 2-bedroom homes were competitive, selling slightly above list on average.
  • Median DOM of 12 days means well-priced homes can still move quickly.
  • Higher-end segments may offer more room for strategy, especially if listings have been sitting.

Key Market Highlights

  • 50 closed sales out of 92 total listings.
  • $507,500 median sold price countywide.
  • 3-bedroom homes led the month with 34 sales and a median sold price of $532,450.
  • 2-bedroom homes achieved the strongest sold-price-to-list-price ratio at 100.04%.
  • 18 expired listings suggest that pricing strategy still matters, even in an active market.
