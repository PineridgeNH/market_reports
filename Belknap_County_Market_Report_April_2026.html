Market Takeaway

A quick read before the numbers.

Belknap County’s April numbers suggest an active spring market with homes selling at 96.45% of listed price countywide. The 3-bedroom category produced the most closed sales, while 1-bedroom homes had the strongest sold-price-to-list-price ratio at 100.00%. The median time on market was 26 days, indicating that well-positioned homes could still move quickly.

What This Means for Sellers

  • Pricing accuracy matters: homes sold at 96.45% of listed price countywide.
  • Three-bedroom homes were the largest source of closed sales this month.
  • Market time varied by segment, so presentation and launch strategy remain important.

What This Means for Buyers

  • Median DOM of 26 days suggests desirable homes may still move quickly.
  • One-bedroom homes were especially competitive based on sold-price-to-list-price ratio.
  • Higher-price segments may offer more room for strategy depending on time on market and seller motivation.
