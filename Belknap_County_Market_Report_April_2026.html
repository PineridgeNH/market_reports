Market Takeaway

A quick read before the numbers.

April’s Belknap County market points to active buyer demand, but with a wider spread by price point and bedroom count. 3-bedroom homes produced the most closed sales, while 4-bedroom homes posted the strongest sell-through rate. The countywide sold-price-to-list-price ratio of 96.46% suggests many homes are still trading close to asking, though strategic pricing remains important.

What This Means for Sellers

  • Homes are still selling close to asking: the countywide sold-price-to-list-price ratio was 96.46%.
  • 3-bedroom homes remain a core demand segment, accounting for nearly half of month-to-date closed sales.
  • Pricing discipline matters: categories with longer days on market suggest buyers are selective when homes stretch above perceived value.

What This Means for Buyers

  • More listings are available across bedroom counts, giving buyers additional choices compared with the closed-sale count.
  • Median DOM of 26 days indicates well-positioned homes can still move quickly.
  • Larger-home segments may offer more room for strategy, especially where days on market are higher.

Key Market Highlights

  • 32 closed sales from 125 total listed properties.
  • $585,000 median sold price countywide.
  • 3-bedroom homes led the market with 15 closed sales and a median sold price of $505,000.
  • 4-bedroom homes posted the strongest sell-through rate at 36.00%.
  • 5+ bedroom homes had the highest median sold price at $3,438,500, based on 2 closed sales.
  • 6 expired listings point to the continued importance of pricing and positioning.
